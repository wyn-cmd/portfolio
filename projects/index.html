Thirty-eight things I have built and shipped

Most of these started as a question I could not answer, or a task I was tired of doing by hand. Security work comes first, then the machine learning and agent work I do both at SQL VIEW and on my own time, then the tooling and coursework that taught me how the pieces fit together. Every entry links to the source.
